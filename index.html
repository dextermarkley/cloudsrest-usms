Registration and participation

U.S. Masters Swimming connects adult swimmers across the country. Clouds Rest welcomes adults of every ability, and we plan to offer adult learn-to-swim lessons.

Register with U.S. Masters Swimming online and choose Clouds Rest as your club during registration. You can also view our club listing before joining.

Meet the coach

Coach Rae Rockey

Hi, I’m Coach Rae. I’m the founder of Clouds Rest, Head Coach of the Yosemite High School Swim Team, and a coach with Clovis Swim Club. I’m a USA Swimming and U.S. Masters Swimming coach with 30 years of coaching experience across multiple sports.

My coaching spans age-group swimmers, young adults, and seniors. I enjoy helping each person build confidence in the water, stay active, and reach personal goals. My family is full of swimmers who have reached state and national rankings. Their drive, passion, and dedication continue to inspire me.
